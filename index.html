More About us

Choosing to seek healthcare abroad is an important decision. Finding the right provider, understanding your options and organising travel can sometimes feel complex.

Shalento Medical Journey is here to help simplify the process. We connect patients with established healthcare providers in India and assist with coordinating key aspects of their medical journey. From your initial enquiry through to planning your visit, our aim is to provide clear communication, personalised support and a smoother overall experience.

Because every journey is different, we take the time to understand your requirements and help you explore suitable options.

Transparency About Our Provider Relationships

Shalento Medical Journey may receive referral fees or commissions from some healthcare providers when we introduce or refer patients to them. Where this applies, we are transparent about these commercial relationships. Our service is designed to help you explore and coordinate healthcare options, but the decision about whether and where to receive treatment always remains yours.
