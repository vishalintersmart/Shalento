About Shalento

Connecting You to Healthcare in India, with Care at Every Step.

Shalento Medical Journey is a UK-based healthcare coordination company helping individuals and families explore healthcare options in India. Through personalised guidance, clear communication and practical travel support, we aim to make international healthcare journeys simpler and more organised.

Who we are

Making Healthcare Journeys Across Borders Simpler.

Exploring medical treatment abroad involves more than choosing a hospital. It requires reliable information, careful planning and support with the practical arrangements that come with travelling to another country.

At Shalento Medical Journey Ltd, we aim to simplify this experience by connecting individuals and families with healthcare providers in India, with a particular focus on Kerala and South India. From initial enquiries and healthcare provider coordination to appointment scheduling, accommodation and local transportation, we assist with organising the important steps of each journey.

We understand that every patient’s circumstances are different. That’s why our approach focuses on individual requirements, transparent communication and personalised coordination, helping patients and accompanying family members navigate their healthcare journey with greater clarity and confidence.

Our Role in Your Healthcare Journey

Shalento Medical Journey supports patients through healthcare journey coordination and practical travel arrangements. We facilitate communication and appointments with independent healthcare providers. All medical assessments, diagnoses, treatment recommendations and clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the relevant healthcare professionals and providers.

Our mission & vision

Our Purpose. Our Commitment.

  • Our Mission

  • Our Vision
